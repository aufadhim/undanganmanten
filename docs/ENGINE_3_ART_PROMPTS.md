# Prompt aset Riviera — landscape panjang

Dibuat dengan tool imagegen bawaan. Gambar referensi: `frontend/public/templates/riviera/assets/river-world.webp`. Hasil proyek dikompresi menjadi WebP 1024 × 1024; tidak meregangkan rasio gambar.

## garden

Lokasi: `frontend/public/templates/riviera/assets/landscape-garden.webp`

Use case: illustration-story. Create a NEW square watercolor environment painting, one tile in a much longer continuous wedding invitation landscape. The supplied image is a STYLE AND PALETTE REFERENCE only. Match its hand-painted cotton paper texture, loose watercolor/gouache brush marks, soft sage olive greenery, dusty pink and ivory flowers, pale sandstone, pale blue water, muted peach light. No photorealism, no 3D rendering, no text, no people, no border, no panels. Spacious pale sky in upper 40%, horizon at 45%, scenery lower 55%. Distant pale blue hills consistent across the scene. Far left and far right edges should be softly painted open vegetation, pale hills and open riverbank, without strong vertical tree trunks or cropped architecture; these edges will blend into adjoining landscape tiles. Fully painted square image. Design the main landmark SMALL and centered within the middle 30% of the image width, so its ENTIRE outline stays readable when the middle third is cropped to a portrait phone screen. Large foreground objects only at the outer sides. A secluded romantic garden clearing at dawn. A pale stone walking path leads from lower center into a modest rose-covered garden pergola in the central middle distance. Soft low flower beds, cypress trees and an old olive tree at the outer far left. A gentle stream begins on the right, flowing out of frame to the right. Keep a quiet open central clearing for a couple illustration. The pergola must be completely visible inside the central 30% width. Light warm ivory sky with a few blush cloud washes.
