## Continuous park panorama → park-world.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one continuous panoramic watercolor background for a cheerful romantic wedding invitation, landscape aspect ratio 3:1, ideally 3072 x 1024. Primary request: a hand painted playful park inspired by a prewedding outing playing soap bubbles, walking together on terracotta steps, picnic and a small garden party. Make a continuous left-to-right world, NOT panels or a collage. Far left: tall leafy tree framing a spacious grassy clearing with tiny wildflowers and an ivory stone footpath where a couple can later be composited. Second quarter: curved low burnt-orange terracotta amphitheater steps and salmon walls with blue/ivory checkerboard paving, gentle grassy banks. Third quarter: open picnic lawn with striped coral/ivory blanket, little wicker basket and vintage bicycle at one side, small stream and low stone crossing. Far right: open lawn under an old tree, pastel ribbon bunting and warm string lights, pale peach evening sky. All zones connected by winding paths and grass. Composition: horizon at upper third, abundant softly painted sky and empty quiet light areas above main scenery for live UI text; readable landmark silhouettes in each quarter; floor near lower third for placing people. No people, no lettering, no cards, no borders, no butterflies or bubbles painted in sky (these animate separately). Style: authentic watercolor and gouache on ivory cold press paper, visible paper tooth, irregular wet edges, broken pigment, loose botanical brushstrokes, slightly whimsical editorial illustration, NOT photorealistic or 3D render. Colors joyful but dusty and moderate: terracotta, butter yellow, powder blue, grass green, blush, restrained navy details. No sharp neon saturation, no palaces, no monumental gates. One consistent daylight moving gently into warm afternoon, no hard zone boundaries. This is a production landscape art asset, not a website mockup.
